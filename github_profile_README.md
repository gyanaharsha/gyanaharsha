## About Me

I'm a cybersecurity practitioner based in India with an M.S. in Computer Science from **Syracuse University** — where I studied Cryptography, Cybersecurity, and Data Science — and a B.Tech in CSE from **Amrita School of Engineering**.

I'm currently focused on building real, hands-on security operations experience through self-directed lab work. Most people with my academic background stay theoretical. I decided to go the other way — build an enterprise-grade lab, simulate real attacks, detect them, respond to them, and document every step of it in public.

The long-term goal is to build a company. Security feels like the right foundation: the problem space is massive, the technical depth is genuine, and it teaches you to think like both an attacker and a defender. That mindset travels well beyond cybersecurity.
